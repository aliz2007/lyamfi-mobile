Lyamfi makes the Casablanca Stock Exchange accessible to new and curious investors.

FOLLOW THE MARKET
• All 80 listings, live quotes and daily changes
• MASI and MASI 20, sectors, fundamentals and shareholders
• Home-screen widgets for indices, movers, watchlist and market status
• Financial news linked to the companies it mentions

LEARN
• 14 progressive modules with quizzes and badges
• A glossary and explanations grounded in the Moroccan market
• A daily investing principle on your home screen

PRACTISE WITHOUT RISK
• A virtual 100,000 MAD portfolio
• Market and limit orders tied to the trading session
• General leaderboard and private leagues
• Investment, inflation and credit simulators

Lyamfi is an educational tool: it executes no real-money trades and provides no personalised investment advice. The interface is available in French, English and Arabic.
